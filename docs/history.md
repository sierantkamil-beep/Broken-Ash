# Broken Ash — history

What each milestone actually built. The plan is `docs/design.md` §11.

### B0 — Scaffold ✅
- **Monorepo** (pnpm: shared, server, client, desktop) on Dinusie's stack. Ported from Dinusie:
  embedded Colyseus server (`startServer`), the Electron shell with UPnP/CGNAT host report and the
  `app://` protocol, the Windows build workflow, the host report UI and address parsing.
- **Free isometric movement** (`shared/src/movement.ts`): screen-relative WASD, acceleration with
  weight, dodge roll (ease-out burst, i-frames, cooldown, stamina), circle-vs-grid collision with
  sliding and anti-tunnelling sub-steps. Deterministic, so it runs on the server and in client
  prediction alike.
- **Netcode**: fixed 60 Hz server step, input frames with sequence numbers, input credit against
  speed hacks, client prediction with reconciliation and smoothed corrections, partner interpolation.
- **Cinematics**: seekable timeline data (camera keys, letterbox, fades, title cards, subtitles,
  actors, events), synced via room state so a late joiner sees the same moment, skip needs both
  votes. The **prolog** (54 s) over the Soramei courtyard.
- **Map**: *Dziedziniec Soramei* (30 × 30) written as code: paths, plaza, gravel garden, koi pond,
  shrine, walls, vermilion gate, lanterns, blossom trees, bamboo, the Greyed dead in their blood.
- **Art pipeline** (`tools/art`): Blender-rendered props (gate, stone and paper lanterns, three
  blossom trees, shrine, walls, rocks, bamboo, Soramei banner, Greyed corpses) with soft contact
  shadows; two convict elves (8 directions × idle/run/roll) from rigid-part models; numpy ground
  painter (flagstones, moss with fallen petals, raked gravel, dirt, water) with seamless 4×4
  super-tiles and feathered borders; painted blood decals; fx sprites (lights, glow, petals, fog).
- **Audio** (`tools/audio`): synthesised in-sen night theme, wind ambience, footsteps on stone/moss,
  dodge, gate impacts, the tree's splitting, gong, UI sounds.
- **Client**: title screen over a drifting view of the courtyard, night lighting with flickering
  lanterns, bloom/vignette, falling petals, drifting fog, props that fade when you walk behind them,
  name tags, stamina bar, area toast, Esc menu (volumes, gore level, raids setting).

### B1 — Characters, all eight paths, the gate assault (in progress → done when the render finishes)
- **Graphify + catalogue**: `main` holds the project so Graphify indexes it; `docs/catalogue.md` maps every
  file and is regenerated by `pnpm catalogue` (a test fails when stale).
- **Characters rebuilt on MakeHuman (CC0)**: elven morph (lean, tall, long ears) for a male and a female
  body, the MakeHuman skeleton and weights, our own outfits per path (lamellar and half-mask, bark plate,
  court robes, shrine robes with talismans, wind robes and war-fans, grave shroud and spirit lantern,
  steppe leathers with fur and an asymmetric bow, night-runner wraps), strand hair, weapons in the hands.
  Skeletal animation library: idle, run, roll, hit, death, and per path an attack and two skill motions.
  The Greyed (Shambler, Runner, Bloated) and the Tapper with his sap tank and cleaver on the same rig.
  16 player sets + 4 Greyed sets, 8 directions, WebP atlases loaded on demand.
- **Combat**: skills as data (cone/line/circle/projectile/self + damage, statuses, dashes, knockback,
  zones, summons, lifesteal, detonations); eight paths × six skills with their own resources (Fury,
  Stamina, Health, Heat with overheating, Charge from moving, Ash from the dead, Focus, Poison doses),
  self-sustain and co-op combos; poise and stagger, executions (F) that heal both players, downed and
  revive, wipes that restart the wave.
- **The Greyed**: flow-field pathing, ground telegraphs that fill as the blow nears, the Bloated's poison
  burst, the Tapper's chop / sweep / slam. **The gate assault**: after the prolog, four waves through
  Soramei's vermilion gate, the Tapper last; victory, then the night starts over (test loop).
- **Client**: character creation (body, people, path), HUD (health with shield, path resource, six-slot
  skill bar with cooldowns and costs, statuses, partner frame, boss bar, downed overlay), mouse aim with
  camera lead, skill visuals per skill, bolts, zones, lightning, blood sprays and persistent pools, gibs on
  overkill, corpses that stay, floating damage numbers, red hurt vignette, fight music.
- **Audio**: 40 new synthesised sounds (blades, gore, fire, thunder, spirits, bows, poison, roots, Greyed
  groans, the Tapper's roar, war horn, revive/downed, execution, victory) and a drum-driven fight theme.
- **Balance simulation** (`server/src/game/sim.test.ts`): scripted bots; every path's sustained damage
  within ±30 % of the average; four pairs win the assault.

### Desktop only, Windows build fixed
- **Windows build**: the catalogue test resolved the repo root with `URL.pathname` (`D:\\D:\\...` on
  Windows) and compared CRLF checkouts with LF output; now `fileURLToPath`, line endings normalised,
  byte-order sorting. The empty `main.yml` workflow (failing every push) is removed.
- **No browser game**: `dev:web` and the browser instructions are gone; `pnpm dev` runs the desktop app.
  Automated checks drive two real Electron windows (`pnpm e2e`): one hosts with the embedded server,
  the other joins, both skip the prolog and fight.
- Fixed: Greyed whose sprite atlas hadn't loaded yet reported position (0, 0) to aiming and effects.

### PixelLab bodies
- **Art source switched to PixelLab.** One male and one female elf body (plain grey tunic, brown
  leather) generated once and shared by every path; a path's look recolours cloth and leather from an
  exact palette (the animations are snapped to it), so armour colours show on the character. Gear
  add-ons (weapons, helmets, plates) will be drawn on top later.
- Motion from PixelLab templates (cleaner loops than custom text animations at this size): run
  (`running-6-frames`), strike (`surprise-uppercut`, a rising draw-and-cut), cast (`fireball`), five
  directions each, mirrored to eight. Idle, hit, death and the dodge roll are derived for free.
- Client: atlases describe themselves (pixel art, scale, frame counts, height); 2× nearest-filtered
  pixels with whole-pixel zoom; melee paths' skills use the strike, magic and ranged the cast.
- Budget: 40 trial generations; a rejected Thorn concept (kept for an NPC) and a rejected custom
  sword-slash test are the only waste.

### Weapons, visible gear, shadow-ink, one pixel style, a real menu
- **Weapon mastery.** Nine weapon types (paired blades, glaive, greatblade, blood brush, talismans,
  war-fan, spirit lantern, longbow, throwing knives), each with a tree: basic attack, RMB (1 of 2),
  key 1 (1 of 3), five passive rows. Any path wields any weapon; the bar is weapon 3 + path 3
  (signature skills). Home weapons: +15 % and the path's rider. Mastery grows from weapon-skill damage,
  is saved on the host (`Profiles`), and the tree is picked in game (K) between waves. Balance sim
  covers paths with home weapons and every weapon off-path.
- **Gear on the body.** Per-frame anchors (detector for head, neck, torso and shoulders; hands
  picked by eye on numbered candidates for strike/cast/idle); weapons, six head pieces, shoulder
  plates, fur mantle and capes as hand-placed pixel parts; looks with 16 dyes, 5 skin tones and armour
  materials (lamellar, scale, silk, leather, bark) drawn into the tunic in every frame.
- **Shadow-ink skills.** A brush-dab painter (ink core, broken rice-paper rim, path accent) and
  compositions for every skill shape; drawn above the night lighting, pre-painted per bar; ink skill
  icons on the HUD and in menus.
- **One pixel style.** The Blender props, ground and Greyed go through a pixel-art pass (art
  resolution, per-frame palettes, soft-shadow alpha step, dark outlines).
- **Menu.** Title screen with keyboard navigation, saved characters, three-column creation with
  full path and weapon explanations and a live paper-doll preview, lobby for host/join.

### Visual reset: top-down, gear locked, exact skill shapes
The visual layer had gone wrong (gear jumping between hands, ink that didn't match the hits), so it
was reset before anything new is added.
- **Top-down 3/4 view** instead of isometric: square 64 px cells (`shared/src/view.ts`), WASD straight
  along the screen (shared `stepBody`, so server and prediction agree), round ground shapes, the ground
  cut into squares, the courtyard re-laid (the south edge closed by low rocks so nothing tall hides
  the players). The PixelLab bodies were already "high top-down". The balance bot now sidesteps
  walls when backing off (backing straight into the south rocks pinned it).
- **Gear causes found and fixed:** mirrored directions (NE/E/SE) measured anchors from the source
  frame's pivot, putting all gear up to ~50 px off the body; weapons now flip with the body about the
  grip; the run has hand picks (the weapon stays in hand, at a steady carry angle) instead of jumping
  to the back on every start and stop; strike and cast blades point out from the torso (measured from
  the nearer shoulder they flipped ~180° mid-swing); head, neck and torso are median-steadied and the
  shoulders follow the neck; the cape swings as a pendulum; the armour pattern keeps one origin per
  clip; a cast's facing survives reconciliation; sharp remote turns switch instead of sweeping. A test
  (`tools/pixellab/anchors.test.ts`) guards the mirrors, the run and the head track.
- **Skill visuals are the exact hit shapes** in the path accent (`client/src/fx/shapes.ts`, shared with
  the telegraphs); bolts are dots of their real radius and end where the server stopped them
  (`boltEnd`). The painted ink compositions are gone; the ink painter still draws the skill icons.
- Still to do: the three east-facing clips are mirrors, so a strike there is left-handed (true
  east-facing clips need PixelLab generations: 2 left of 40); map props and the Greyed are still
  isometric renders; walk speed, ranges and the knives/bow balance shift (68→111 / 79→65 in the
  sim) need a pass after a playtest.

### Visual audit, part 2: camera, map art, icons, HUD, menus
An audit of every screen (title, creation, lobby, HUD, mastery, prolog, fight) found what still
looked broken and why; all of it was fixed.
- **Camera:** bounds on the map and a minimum zoom, so there is never black void past the edge;
  play zoom crisp and never under 1:1 (characters stay 124 px); the title drift and the prolog
  re-framed for the top-down map (they were tuned for the twice-as-wide iso map).
- **Map art re-rendered top-down** in Blender: props, walls (straight one-cell segments that tile),
  shrine, gate, lanterns, corpses, blood pools and both Greyed, through a 35° camera stretched so the
  ground is square; the Greyed scaled to stand as tall as the players; `Z_PX` follows the art.
- **Skill icons are crisp pixel art** built from data: the skill's shape (crescent, bar, bolt,
  target, burst, pool, chevron) plus a badge for its main effect, on an accent plate, 24 px at 2×.
  They replace the ink icons (unreadable speckle) in the HUD, menus, mastery and lobby. A pixel
  title mark (an enso around the split World-Ash) sits beside the name instead of under it; weapon
  cards show whole-pixel weapon icons.
- **HUD:** the stamina ticks no longer cross the whole HUD (two stray black lines); key badges,
  names in tooltips, taller bars, key hints fade after a minute.
- **Mastery panel:** chosen / locked by level / "not now" look different; sticky header, a scroll
  fade, the HUD steps back while it's open.
- Polish letters in the heading font (latin-ext); colour and spacing tokens in the stylesheet.

### Pixel spell effects (Phase 1 of "spectacle, creatures, gore, Act 1")
- Every skill has a real effect over its exact hit shape, built from data: element from the weapon or
  path, look from the shape and vfx key; signature skills have hand-made recipes (Piorun is a real
  forking bolt with a flash of the screen, Stos a fireball blast and a burning field, Powstańcie
  ghost hands clawing out of the ground, Nawałnica lightning out of the sky…).
- `tools/fx/build.py` bakes the small sprites (sparks, flames, clouds, roots, hands, projectiles,
  status loops) in eleven element palettes; the client generates big shapes (blade smears, whirls,
  shockwave rings, seals, blasts, cyclones) at the exact size each skill needs, so every effect keeps
  the characters' 2× pixel size. Pixel art draws opaque with its dark rim; only light adds.
- Feel: pixel particles (embers, sparks, dust), effects light the night, hit-stop and a knock-back
  kick on heavy blows, shake, screen flashes for lightning. Projectiles fly as arrows, knives,
  burning talismans or wisps with trails and burst on impact where the server stopped them.
- Statuses show on bodies: flames (burn), bubbles (poison), drips (bleed), sparks (static), vines
  (rooted), a sigil over a marked head, stars (stagger), a shield dome.
- Server: hits carry the attacker, the blow's direction and how it was dealt (cut, crush, fire,
  shock…), detonations are flagged, deaths carry direction, cause and overkill (for gore next),
  enemy blows send their own event.

### Gore (Phase 2)
- Blood flies the way the blow went, lands as splats and stays: one blood layer under the lighting
  (art resolution, the whole map) keeps every drop, pool, smear and settled part for the visit.
- Bodies break the way they were killed: crushing blows burst them (torso, head, limbs, ribs, guts,
  bone shards that tumble, bounce, slide and smear), cuts behead or take an arm with the neck pumping,
  executions always tear the body; fire chars the corpse and leaves embers, lightning blackens it
  with smoke, rot melts it into a green pool, spirits drain it pale.
- Executions: a held heartbeat on both fighters, a camera punch-in, a red flash, heavy shake.
- Gib sprites drawn as pixel art in `tools/fx/build.py` (Greyed ash skin and human skin); the
  reduced-gore setting keeps sprays only. `tools/e2e/gore.cjs` photographs each kind of death.
